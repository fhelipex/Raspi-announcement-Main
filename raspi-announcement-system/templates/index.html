🎙️ Enabling Microphone on Local Network

Modern browsers block microphone access on insecure connections (like `http://192.168.x.x`). You can bypass this by instructing your browser to treat this local address as secure.

For Google Chrome & Microsoft Edge:

  1. Open a new tab in your browser and enter:
    chrome://flags/#unsafely-treat-insecure-origin-as-secure (Chrome)
    or
    edge://flags/#unsafely-treat-insecure-origin-as-secure (Edge)
  2. Locate the flag: "Insecure origins treated as secure".
  3. Set the dropdown option to Enabled.
  4. In the text area provided, paste the URL of this node:
    http://YOUR-IP:PORT
  5. If configuring the other node, you can add its URL as well (separated by a comma).
  6. Click Relaunch at the bottom right of the browser.

Once relaunched, you will be able to grant microphone permissions normally and use the intercom over your WiFi network!
